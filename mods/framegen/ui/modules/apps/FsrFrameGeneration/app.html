Calibration

BeamNG documents neither its depth nor its motion-vector encoding. These correct it — turn on the debug view to check them against AMD's own visualisation.

Direct3D 12 cannot be asked what state a texture is in, and BeamNG does not document what its PostFX system leaves one in. FSR transitions the beacon targets from whatever is declared here. If the image is wrong or the log reports barrier errors, this is the setting to try.

Debugging

Compatibility mode disables async compute and version pinning without discarding either setting. Try it before reporting a crash.
